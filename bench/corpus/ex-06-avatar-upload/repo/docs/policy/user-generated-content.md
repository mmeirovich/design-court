# User-generated content: launch requirements

Applies to any feature that shows files, images or text uploaded by one user to other users.

Before launch, the feature's design must record:

1. A decision from the Trust & Safety lead on how content is moderated: reviewed before it
   becomes visible, reviewed after (for example through reports), or not reviewed.
2. A decision from the Trust & Safety lead on how long replaced or removed content is retained
   before it is deleted, and who may access it during that time.
3. The names of the people who sign off on both.

Reports are triaged by the T&S team through the abuse-report queue.
